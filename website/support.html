Support

Need help with Jott?

If you run into issues with purchase restore, slash commands, or note behavior, contact us and include your macOS version plus a short screen recording.

Contact

  • Email: support@jott.app
  • Response target: within 2 business days

Troubleshooting

  • Purchase not unlocked: open paywall and tap “Restore Purchases”.
  • Offerings not loading: check DNS/VPN/network settings and relaunch app.
  • Slash command not recognized: start input with / and command name.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple through your App Store account purchase history.
