Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2026

Jott is designed to keep your notes workflow simple. This policy explains what data may be processed when you use the app and website.

Data we process

  • Account and purchase status provided by Apple / RevenueCat for entitlement checks.
  • Basic diagnostic events needed to troubleshoot purchase and restore issues.
  • Support emails you send to us.

Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat is used to validate purchase state and deliver entitlement status across app sessions.
